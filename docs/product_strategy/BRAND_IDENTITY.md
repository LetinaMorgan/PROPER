# Brand Identity Guidelines: Proper

## 1. Brand Essence & Core Foundation

### Brand Purpose
To empower independent local service experts with software that turns their hard work into a structured, highly professional business.

### Core Promise
**"Run a Proper Business."**  
Eliminating the friction of SMS back-and-forth, no-shows, and admin headaches so service pros can focus on craftsmanship and growth.

### Taglines

#### Primary Tagline
* **"Run a Proper business."** (Direct, aspirational, authoritative)

#### Secondary / Feature Taglines
* **"Service booking, done properly."** (Marketplace / Client-facing)
* **"Less texting. More wrenching."** (For mobile mechanics/detailers)
* **"Zero back-and-forth. 100% booked."** (Utility / Efficiency angle)

---

## 2. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

Proper sits right at the intersection of **craftsman grit** and **modern software polish**. It is not overly corporate, nor is it cheap or casual.

| Attribute | What It Means | How It Sounds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct & No-BS** | Clear, concise, and respects the user's time. | *"Set your rates. Share your link. Get paid."* |
| **Grounded & Respectful** | Values hard work, trades, and expertise. | *"You built the skill. We build the operating system."* |
| **Sharp & Confident** | Professional without being stiff or overly academic. | *"Never chase a deposit again."* |
| **Relieving** | Removes operational anxiety for solo operators. | *"Your schedule is locked. Your deposits are secured."* |

## 3. Brand Messaging Grid

### For Service Experts (The SaaS User)
* **Hook:** *"Stop losing billing hours to Instagram DMs and text tax."*
* **Core Value:** A mobile-first booking & scheduling tool built specifically for mobile pros who travel to client locations.
* **Key Benefits:**
  * **Deposit Protection:** Automatically capture 20% upfront to eliminate no-shows.
  * **Location-Aware Buffers:** Smart drive-time routing so you're never running late.
  * **Instant Pro Link:** A sleek booking link (`proper.app/joesdetailing`) for your bio.

### For End Clients (The Consumer)
* **Hook:** *"Book vetted local experts in under 60 seconds."*
* **Core Value:** Real-time availability, upfront pricing, and instant confirmation without phone tag.
* **Key Benefits:**
  * Upfront transparent pricing—no bait-and-switch.
  * Automated SMS dispatch alerts ("On my way").
  * Instant deposit & checkout security.

## 4. Visual Identity Direction

### Color Palette

#### Primary Palette
* **Deep Steel Charcoal (`#0F172A`)**: Represents strength, modern tech background, and high structure.
* **Proper Emerald / Mint (`#10B981`)**: Represents growth, instant payouts, precision, and green-light status.
* **Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: High contrast, clean readability.

#### Accent Palette
* **Industrial Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Used sparingly for high-trust badges, star ratings, and callouts.
* **Cool Gray Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Secondary text, borders, and UI structure.

### Typography System
* **Primary Display Font:** `Outfit` or `Inter` (Bold, tight tracking for headlines, conveying modern precision).
* **Body Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto` (Clean, legible across small mobile screens).

### Logo Concept & Symbol Direction
* **Symbol Concept:** A stylized checkmark integrated into a square/frame or a geometric **"P"** that doubles as a pin/calendar grid.
* **Visual Vibe:** Sleek, sharp edges, modern app icon, premium dark-mode aesthetic with vibrant accent lighting.
